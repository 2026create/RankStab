Many grocers have made great progress on analytical maturity. Leaders in analytics have tackled the majority of fundamental use cases, such as pricing, mass promotion, and assortment optimization. Now they have increasingly turned their focus to pursuing new use cases along the value chain and improving existing use cases-for example, by using more granular, realtime data. These efforts are often driven by a strong analytics unit, but the level of adoption across a business varies.
