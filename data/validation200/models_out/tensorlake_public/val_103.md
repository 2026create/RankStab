The image displays a line graph with the following details:

Legend and Data Lines:
a 去顶芽，切除涂抹赤霉素 (Remove apical bud, cut and apply gibberellin): This line shows a steep increase in average bud length over time.
b 去顶芽 (Remove apical bud): This line also shows an increase but at a slower rate compared to line "a".
c 顶芽保留，侧芽上涂细胞分裂素 (Apical bud retained, lateral buds treated with cytokinin): This line indicates a moderate increase in average bud length.
d 去顶芽，切除涂抹生长素 (Remove apical bud, cut and apply auxin): This line shows a slight increase in average bud length.
e 顶芽保留 (Apical bud retained): This line remains relatively flat, indicating minimal change in average bud length.

Key Observations from the Graph:
The highest average bud length is achieved by removing the apical bud and applying gibberellin ("a").
Retaining the apical bud results in the lowest average bud length ("e").
Applying cytokinin to lateral buds when the apical bud is retained ("c") leads to a moderate increase in bud length.
Other treatments involving removal of the apical bud result in varying degrees of growth enhancement, with auxin application showing less effect than gibberellin.
