Family Preservation and Assessment System
Hillsborough County; Florida
Summary
Hillsborough County's Family Preservation and Assessment System is
county-wide data-sharing platform in the children services sector. The
purpose of the Family Preservation and Assessment System to enable
the unification of multiple sources of data regarding families with child at
risk ofbeing abused or neglected Or child in care. Hillsborough County;
in collaboration With EY, has designed this tool to help realize the vision of
preservation of families through targeted early intervention and support。
The tool has been deployed in the community since March 2021.
